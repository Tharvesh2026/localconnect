<h2 style="color: #1e3a5f; font-size: 24px;">📌 About the Project</h2>

<p style="font-size: 15px; line-height: 1.6; color: #a8a2a2;">
  In many towns and villages, people struggle to find reliable local workers like electricians, plumbers, tailors, or tutors.
  They usually rely on word of mouth, and there's no centralized, trustworthy list.
  <br /><br />
  <strong>LocalConnect</strong> solves this with a simple web app where local service providers can register,
  and residents can search, filter, and contact them directly — with a review system to build trust over time.
</p>
